# Task 11 – Business Performance Analysis Using Bubble Chart

## Objective

Analyze and compare different business categories using a Bubble Chart and other visualizations in Tableau Public to identify significant and low-performing business categories.

## Problem Statement

A business-related dataset containing categorical and numerical fields was analyzed using Tableau Public. A Bubble Chart was created to compare business categories based on Sales and Profit. Additional visualizations and an interactive filter were added to provide a complete view of business performance.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset
**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains business information such as:

* Category
* Sub-Category
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Region
* Segment
* Order Date

## Tools Used

* Tableau Public
* Kaggle Dataset
* Microsoft Excel / CSV

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Bubble Chart

The Bubble Chart compares different sub-categories.

* **Bubble Size:** Sales
* **Bubble Color:** Profit
* **Label:** Sub-Category
* **Detail:** Sub-Category

Larger bubbles represent higher sales, while the color indicates the profitability of each sub-category.

### 2. Category Sales Bar Chart

A bar chart was created to compare total sales across different categories.

### 3. Regional Performance Chart

A visualization was created to compare sales and profit across different regions.

### 4. Sales Trend Chart

A line chart was created to analyze sales performance over time using Order Date.

## Interactive Filter

A **Region** filter was added to the dashboard.

Users can select different regions such as:

* Central
* East
* South
* West
* All

The visualizations update dynamically according to the selected region.

## Dashboard

All four visualizations were combined into a single interactive Tableau dashboard.

The dashboard helps users compare:

* Sales
* Profit
* Product categories
* Regional performance
* Sales trends

## Business Insights

1. Different sub-categories contribute different levels of sales and profit to the overall business.

2. Higher sales do not always indicate higher profitability, so both Sales and Profit should be considered when evaluating business performance.

3. Business performance varies across regions, showing differences in sales and profitability between geographical areas.

4. The Bubble Chart helps identify sub-categories with high sales and strong profitability as well as areas that require further investigation.

5. The sales trend visualization helps identify changes in business performance over time.

## Business Recommendations

1. The business should focus on maintaining and promoting products that consistently generate strong sales and profit.

2. Sub-categories with high sales but comparatively low profit should be investigated for pricing, discounting, shipping costs, and other factors affecting profitability.
